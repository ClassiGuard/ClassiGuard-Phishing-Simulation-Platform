## 4. Ethical Simulation Guidelines

When running authorized security awareness tests, operators must maintain ethical guardrails:

* **Zero Credential and PII Harvesting:** Never configure the tool to log, store, or exfiltrate real plaintext passwords, MFA tokens, financial data, or sensitive personal information. Only track basic simulation telemetry (e.g., whether a link was clicked or a simulation form was triggered) for educational feedback.
* **Prohibited Lure Themes:** Do not create phishing templates that exploit high-trauma or cruel pretexts, including:
* Layoffs, terminations, or disciplinary notices.
* Medical emergencies, bereavement, or public health crises.
* Disasters, emergencies, or fake financial bonuses.


* **Education Over Shaming:** Use simulation results purely to help individuals learn how to spot threats—never to publicly shame, punish, or trick people maliciously.
* **Infrastructure Cleanup:** Immediately shut down and take offline any simulation servers, landing pages, or test domains once your authorized test is complete so they cannot be abused by third parties.
