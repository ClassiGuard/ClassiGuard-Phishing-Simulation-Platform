## 4. Ethical Simulation Guidelines

When running authorized security awareness tests, operators must maintain strict ethical guardrails:

* **Zero Credential and PII Harvesting:** Never configure the tool to log, store, or exfiltrate real plaintext passwords, MFA tokens, financial data, or sensitive personal information. Only track basic simulation telemetry (e.g., whether a simulated lure was clicked or reported) for educational feedback.
* **Prohibited Lure Themes:** Do not create phishing templates that exploit traumatic or malicious pretexts, including:
  * Layoffs, terminations, or disciplinary actions.
  * Medical emergencies, bereavement, or public health crises.
  * Disasters, physical threats, or fraudulent financial bonuses.
* **Education Over Shaming:** Use simulation results purely to help individuals learn how to recognize threats—never to publicly shame, punish, or harass employees.
* **Infrastructure Cleanup:** Immediately decommission any simulation servers, landing pages, or test domains once your authorized exercise is complete so they cannot be abused by third parties.
